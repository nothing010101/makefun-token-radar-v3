How to read this radar

  • Implied value is the entry's own claim, never make.fun's. It is TVL × the market base (the median market-cap/TVL of the protocols in this snapshot whose token already trades) × a scale factor ((TVL / $32.6M)^−0.50, fitted on those 128 live protocols: market cap grows like the square root of TVL) × a sector factor (the protocol's own sector median, shrunk toward the market with a 60-protocol prior) × momentum (this week's change against the week's median, winsorised and tanh-bounded, weighted 0.2 in the exponent — the live peers show no reliable link between one week's growth and the multiple, so growth is a nudge, not a verdict) × a track-record factor (log age, 0.90–1.10) × a reach factor (log chains, capped at nine).
  • Bands compare the implied multiple to the market base. A is ≥ 1.5× the base, B ≥ 1.0×, C ≥ 0.6×, else D. Small protocols land higher because live tokens trade richer at small size; that is the fitted scale effect, not a bonus for being small.
  • “Records no token” is not “has no token.” The snapshot is one DefiLlama fetch; a protocol on the board may still have a token its record does not carry. Check before you farm.
  • The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling. Forty protocols clear the $50M floor with no token recorded; the board adds every rankable pre-token protocol below it, so the early finds are the point.
  • Farm plans are plans, not promises. No airdrop is guaranteed, and no step here asks you to pay anyone to claim. Costs are gas plus the capital you choose to deploy.
